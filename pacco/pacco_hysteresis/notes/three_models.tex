\noindent\textbf{Notes.}\;
(i) Model\,1 includes polynomial nonlinearities up to $H^4$ plus the interaction $T\!\cdot\!H$, yielding good hysteresis reproduction at the cost of 52 parameters.
(ii) Model\,2 (ADMM) selects a sparser library (25 parameters) but loses the hysteresis cycle.
(iii) Model\,3 uses bilinear cross-terms ($C\!\cdot\!H$, $B\!\cdot\!C$, $T\!\cdot\!H_s$, $T\!\cdot\!I$) instead of high-degree polynomials, covering the full 2\,Myr attractor set.
$H_s \equiv H_{\mathrm{sed}}$.
